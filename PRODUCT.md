# Browser-Native AI Video Studio — product truth

Browser-Native is a focused desktop production workspace for turning a story brief into an inspectable script, storyboard, media queue, and reviewable edit. It serves creators who need operational clarity more than decoration.

## Product priorities

1. Preserve one readable handoff from brief → story → scene → shot → media → review.
2. Make provider state, errors, retries, and recovery visible without exposing implementation noise.
3. Keep the user's authored story and locked references authoritative; automation may propose or execute, never silently rewrite.
4. Support long-form projects while validating accidental or unreasonable input before planning.
