# Visual Explainer Video

## Story
- Define one audience question and one answer. Arrange scenes as hook, context, demonstration, recap.
- Use concrete examples and observable actions. Do not invent statistics, studies, or certainty.
- Keep each shot responsible for one claim or visual step.

## Prompt and keyframe
- Make the subject, action, and comparison readable without relying on captions.
- Keep diagrams, labels, and interfaces minimal; request readable text only when essential.
- Reuse a stable presenter, object, palette, and camera grammar.

## Video
- Use simple sequential motion that demonstrates the explanation. Avoid unrelated cinematic action.
- Preserve the attached reference exactly and do not introduce unsupported visual claims.

## Review
- Check factual alignment, visual order, legibility, and whether every shot supports the central answer.
