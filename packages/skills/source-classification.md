# Source Classification

Classify source material before adaptation.

- Idea: expand missing story structure without replacing the central premise.
- Novel: preserve chronology, causality, motivation, key dialogue, and convert internal narration into visible action.
- Screenplay: preserve scene order and dialogue intent; normalize rather than reinvent.

Apply the selected delivery format:

- Short film: complete cinematic arc and emotional continuity.
- Short-form video: hook inside three seconds, one central idea, dense progression, strong payoff or loop.
- Video series: episodic hooks and payoffs with a persistent series bible for characters, locations, props, visual rules, and unresolved threads.
