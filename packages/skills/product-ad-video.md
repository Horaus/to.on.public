# Product Ad Video

## Story
- Establish a specific customer situation, product interaction, benefit demonstration, and concise payoff.
- Keep claims supportable and separate product capability from mood or aspiration.

## Identity
- Lock product geometry, materials, logo placement, packaging, color, and scale before keyframes.

## Prompt and keyframe
- Show the product clearly in use. Preserve hero angle and distinguishing details across shots.
- Avoid fake UI, altered packaging, unsupported results, and unreadable brand marks.

## Video
- Use controlled product and camera motion. Do not redesign the attached product reference.

## Review
- Reject geometry, packaging, color, claim, or interaction mismatches.
